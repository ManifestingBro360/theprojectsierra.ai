Add your logo

You can be listed as a company, as a team inside a company, or as a product built with one of the projects.

  1. Open the adopter request form.
  2. Give the name, a link, which project you use, and one sentence on how you use it.
  3. Attach a logo as an SVG, or a PNG at least 400 pixels wide, that you have the right to share.
  4. A maintainer checks the request and adds the logo, usually within a week.

The request must come from someone who can speak for the company, team or product. To change or remove a logo later, open an issue and it is done without questions.
